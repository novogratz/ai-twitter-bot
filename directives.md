**Synthesis:** Named actor + exact number + consequence = 10–40x more likes than any clever one-liner. METAPHOR is the top registered pattern. Quotes are critically underused (59 vs 751 RTs this week) — every RT candidate must become a quote.

## Apply to every tweet/reply/hot take:

- **Actor + number + consequence, or skip.** "Anthropic raising $157B at $965B valuation — first profitable quarter incoming" beats "AI valuations are wild." No named actor + no verifiable figure = do not post.
- **METAPHOR first.** Map news to a recognizable absurd situation: Bercy, 401k Dave, URSSAF, RER B, LinkedIn Liam, Tonton Patrick. "CoreWeave's $7B debt stack + 80% margin — they are the casino dealer that won't let you fold." Lock this pattern before any other.
- **RENAME to reveal the real dynamic.** Strip the PR spin. "SpaceX is a defense contractor that charges admission." "Jensen doesn't sell GPUs — he sells a CUDA tax." One line, no explanation.
- **DIALOGUE only for sharp contrasts.** Two sides each wrong in a different way. "OpenAI wants to be Apple. Anthropic wants to be Adobe." Two lines max, zero hedge.
- **No links in the tweet body.** Drop the source URL as the first reply. Links cut reach 30–50%.
- **Reply velocity is the algorithm.** A reply on a SpaceX tweet within 5 minutes of posting outweighs 10 standalone hot takes. A reply-to-your-reply from the account owner is +75 algo score; a like is +0.5. Be there first.
- **Quote everything, retweet nothing bare.** Add one punchy line to every RT candidate. A quote without an angle is a dressed-up RT — skip it if you have nothing to say.
- **Bold stock call format (use sparingly, max 1/catalyst).** "$RKLB\nNeutron + defense backlog. $30 incoming.\n🚀🚀🚀" — no hedge, no disclaimer, pure conviction. Reserve for real catalysts: ASTS BlueBirds launch mid-June, RKLB Neutron debut, SpaceX IPO.
- **Hot take threshold: laugh-test + specific fact.** Will a human laugh AND learn something? No to either = skip. "SoftBank drops €75B on French AI datacenters — the CAC 40 finally gets a motherboard." Yes. "AI is changing everything." No.
