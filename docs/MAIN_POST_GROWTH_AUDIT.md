# Main-Post Growth Audit

Generated from the repository state on 2026-08-18.

## Current Architecture

The bot is a macOS Safari automation system. `main.py` runs APScheduler jobs that call many small bot modules. Posting and scraping are driven through `src/twitter_client.py`, Safari, AppleScript, and X web pages, not the official X API.

```text
RSS/HN/X search/Home signals
        |
        v
external_signal.json + Safari scrapers
        |
        +--> reply/direct-reply engines --> reply_to_tweet()
        |
        +--> main-post surfaces
             news/hotake, breakout, spicy, hot_quote, thread
                    |
                    v
             content_guard/action_guard/twitter_client
                    |
                    v
                  X via Safari
```

The scheduler has two distinct growth engines:

- Replies: `direct_reply.py`, `reply_bot.py`, `notify_bot.py`, `chain_reply_bot.py`, `feed_sweeper_bot.py`, and related modules. These scrape profile/search/home surfaces and reply under other accounts.
- Main posts: `bot.py` orchestrates news/hotake posts, while `spicy_bot.py`, `breakout_bot.py`, `hot_quote_bot.py`, `thread_bot.py`, and `viral_stunt_bot.py` are independent original/quote surfaces.

## Reply Engine

Replies are selected from:

- fixed and dynamic target accounts in `direct_reply.py`
- X searches in `SEARCH_QUERIES` / `HOT_TAB_QUERIES`
- Home and Following feed scrapes
- notification/replyback surfaces
- discovered/dynamic accounts from `dynamic_strategy.py`

Replies are generated by reply-specific prompts and posted through `twitter_client.reply_to_tweet`. Deduplication is handled with `replied_tweets.json`, age gates, language/niche filters, blocklists, and content guard checks. Reply activity is logged in `engagement_log.csv`.

Why replies appear to work:

- They inherit distribution from existing conversations.
- They target large and mid-size AI/market accounts.
- They are short, reactive, and conversational.
- The style naturally rewards specificity and opinion.
- They serve discovery, profile visits, and audience sensing even when reply impressions are not the 500k Home objective.

This implementation does not broadly redesign the reply engine.

## Main-Post Engine

Main ideas currently originate from:

- `external_signal.json` fed by RSS/HN/X scout modules
- X search/top-tab candidates
- hot quote topics discovered from X
- historical prompt memory from `self_winners.md`, `reply_winners.md`, `performance_insights.json`, `learnings.json`
- hardcoded post slot surfaces in `main.py`

Main posts are produced by several disconnected surfaces:

- `agent.py`: Décode/news prompt, heavily source-pool driven
- `hotake_agent.py`: short AI-market observations
- `spicy_bot.py`: spicy/question posts anchored to fresh signals
- `breakout_bot.py`: fast trend reaction
- `hot_quote_bot.py`: quote posts based on hot X posts
- `thread_bot.py`: daily AI rundown/thread

The current design has useful safeguards but weak editorial unification. It often starts from “source item -> short take” rather than “source facts -> original account insight.”

## Weakness Analysis

Observed risks:

- excessive fragmentation across main-post surfaces
- quote-post dependence for hot stories
- source-dependent reactions that may not stand alone
- many prompts still reward speed and volume over insight depth
- high post-slot count can encourage cron-driven posting instead of expected value per post
- performance learning exists but is blended and shallow
- reply and main-post analytics are not cleanly separated
- official qualified Home Timeline impressions are not available in local data
- account memory exists in several files but not as one main-post editorial memory

Historical local data supports the problem:

- `performance_log.json`: 200 recent main-post metric rows
- median main-post impressions: 46.5
- average main-post impressions: 589.89, skewed by outliers
- p75: 73
- p90: 96
- hit count using rolling median heuristic: 14
- breakout count: 1
- `reply_analytics`: 48,613 reply rows in the 90-day window

The account is producing many interactions in replies but main posts are not consistently earning Home distribution.

## Implemented Direction

Added `src/main_post_growth.py` as a shared main-post intelligence layer:

- separated main-post analytics
- separated reply analytics
- opportunity queue
- account memory
- 500k Home Timeline dashboard
- editorial brief
- prediction ledger placeholder
- experiment definitions
- rewards-aware approval queue

Main-post prompts now receive a compact growth brief. Reply prompts and reply scheduling are unchanged.
